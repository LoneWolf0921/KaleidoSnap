# KaleidoSnap

A cozy, private browser photobooth inspired by Purikura and classic photo strip kiosks. Snap, decorate, and save or print your photo strips right from your phone or computer—your photos never leave your device.

## User Guide

1. **Allow Camera Access** — Open KaleidoSnap and allow camera access to start the live viewfinder.
2. **Choose a Format** — Pick `Single (1×1)`, `Strip (1×3)`, or `Grid (2×2)`. If you switch formats after taking shots, you can choose which photos to keep.
3. **Capture Shots** — Tap **Capture** (or press `Space` / `Enter` on a keyboard) to start the countdown (`3s` by default, adjustable from `1s` to `10s`). Press `Escape` to cancel a countdown or close any window.
4. **Review, Swap & Retake** — Check your photos on the Light Table. Tap any frame to retake just that shot, or turn on **Swap Shots** to reorder them (`#01 ↔ #02`).
5. **Style & Decorate** — Pick a color filter, choose from 12 frame styles, customize your footer caption, font style (`Modern`, `Mono`, `Script`), and date stamp, and decorate with 119 stickers.
   - **Zoom & Pan**: Use `+` / `−`, mouse-wheel/trackpad scroll, or two-finger pinch to zoom up to `300%` and pan around while placing stickers.
   - **Sticker Controls**: Drag to move, use the corner handles to resize, drag the top handle to rotate, or use arrow keys / `+` / `-` / `[` / `]` / `Delete` on desktop.
6. **Download or Print** — Tap **Download PNG** to save a crisp image, or open **Print PDF** to automatically lay out your photos onto standard photo paper (`3R`, `4R`, `5R`, `2×6" Strip`, `A4`, or `US Letter`) with cut guides.

## Features

- **3 Layouts**: `Single (1×1)`, `Strip (1×3)`, and `Grid (2×2)`.
- **6 Color Filters**: `Natural`, `Soft Peach Glow`, `90s Disposable`, `Crisp Mono`, `Matcha Film`, and `Midnight Cyanotype`.
- **12 Frame Presets**: `Classic White`, `Photo Black`, `Scalloped Lace`, `Gingham Check`, `90s Film`, `Botanical Leaf`, `Starry Night`, `Notebook Doodle`, `8-Bit Pixel`, `Pixel Cafe`, `Holo Gradient`, and `Polaroid Tape`.
- **119 Stickers**: Across 11 categories (`Props`, `Food`, `Y2K`, `Nature`, `Scrapbook`, `Expressions`, `Doodles`, `Sparkles`, `Critters`, `Arcade`, `Stamps`).
- **Print-Ready PDF Layouts**: Automatic multi-up sizing and dashed cut guides for `3.5×5" (3R)`, `4×6" (4R)`, `5×7" (5R)`, `2×6" Strip`, `A4 (Landscape)`, and `US Letter (Landscape)`.
- **8 App Themes (with Unique Icons & Sound Effects)**: `Light Paper`, `Dark Studio`, `Pink Neko`, `Midnight Arcade`, `Matcha Cream`, `Lavender Soda`, `8-Bit Retro`, and `Pixel Art Cafe`.
- **Preferences & Auto-Save**: Adjustable countdown (`1s–10s`), date stamp format (`MM.DD.YYYY`, `MM-DD-YYYY`, `DD.MM.YYYY`, `YYYY-MM-DD`, `Mon DD, YYYY`), mirror camera, sound effects, animations, camera selector, and automatic 30-minute local session recovery.
